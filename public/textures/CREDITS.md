The photos of Elon Musk are used for parody. He has no connection with this game and has not endorsed it. NASA's media guidelines say images of identifiable people must not be used to suggest endorsement; the game labels the character as a parody.

How the photos are used: each one is a real photograph with only its background removed. The game moves the whole picture (a bob, a hop, a shake) and draws effects around it. Nothing inside a photo is redrawn, recoloured or reshaped, and none of them shows him doing what the game's story says. The "tantrum" picture is him answering a question at a news conference.
